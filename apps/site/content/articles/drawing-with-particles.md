Every form on this site is a cloud of particles looking for a surface. Each one
is given a point to reach, and a little freedom in how it gets there. The result
is closer to drawing than to rendering: the picture is never finished, only
held.

## Surfaces, not meshes

A shape is described as a distance: how far any point in space is from its
surface. Particles step towards zero. That one idea covers spheres, knots,
gyroids and anything that can be written as a formula.

- Forms are formulas, so they are small to send and easy to change.
- Particles settle onto them, so changing the formula morphs the form.
- The same particles can become text, a line, or a chart.

> The picture is never finished, only held.
